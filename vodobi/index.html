Vodobi Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Learning data and settings

Vodobi is an Android vocabulary learning app. You can use it without creating an account. Your language preferences, study plans, practice history, learning progress, streaks, and achievements are stored on your device so the app can remember your settings and personalize your practice. Vodobi sends no learning records to a developer-operated server.

Android may include eligible app data in a cloud backup or transfer it to a new device, depending on your device and backup settings. You can manage backups in Android settings.

Unlock practice and reminders

When you enable practice after unlocking, Vodobi uses Android's screen and unlock events to decide when to show a vocabulary exercise. Android can restart the background service when the device boots. Whether an exercise appears depends on your Vodobi settings and Android permissions. This processing takes place on your device. Vodobi uses the draw-over-other-apps permission to open the exercise after unlocking, without reading the contents of other apps. Android's notification permission allows Vodobi to show reminders and a service notification. You can manage these features in Vodobi or Android settings.

Advertising and consent

Vodobi uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location, as well as app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. The SDK may also process the app set ID for purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this information in transit.

Vodobi may show a banner in exercises you start in the app. AdMob requests are capped at General Audiences (G). For anyone under 18 or whose age is unknown, Vodobi marks ad requests as child-directed. Google disables interest-based ads and remarketing and does not receive the Android advertising ID in those ad requests. Google may still process technical information such as the IP address to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

Vodobi uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage ad consent where applicable. When available, you can review your choices under Advertising privacy options in Vodobi settings. Android also provides controls for resetting or deleting the advertising ID.

For details about Google's handling and retention of data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Age suitability and children

Vodobi asks for your date of birth when you open the app and have not yet answered the age question. You can skip it and still use the app. Vodobi uses the date on your device to choose an advertising age group. It saves only the age group on that device and does not save or transmit your date of birth. The age group is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. If you skip the question, Vodobi treats your age as unknown and you can enter the date later in Settings. The Google Play listing shows the content rating for your region.

Parents and guardians can contact us at thirdboot.theory@gmail.com if they have questions about a child's data or want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about their child.

Translation reports and support

If you report a translation error, Vodobi opens a draft in your email app. The draft can include the word, its translation, the language pair, the dictionary entry ID, any displayed answer choices, the app version, and an optional comment. You can edit the draft or discard it. If you send it, we receive the contents and your email address and use them to investigate the report or reply. The same applies when you email us for support.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Vodobi keeps local app data to preserve your settings and progress. Use Reset learning data in Vodobi settings to delete progress, practice history, custom plans, and achievements. To remove all local app data, clear Vodobi's storage in Android settings. Manage any Android backups through your device or backup provider.
  • We keep support and translation emails for as long as needed to handle the request, follow up, or meet applicable legal obligations.
  • For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you, contact the Vodobi developer at thirdboot.theory@gmail.com.
